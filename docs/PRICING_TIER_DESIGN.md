Based on actual market pricing, not SaaS fantasy numbers.

**Includes:**
- Transcript management
- Deposition uploads
- Basic review tools
- Standard support

**Target:** Independent court reporters, small agencies

**Value Prop:** Professional transcription platform at affordable price

---

**Includes:**
- Everything in Starter
- Advanced review tools
- Exhibit uploads
- Agency workflow management
- Priority support

---

**Includes:**

## 🔑 PRICING PHILOSOPHY

### Why Attorneys Pay More

1. **Higher Value:** Trial preparation is mission-critical
2. **Higher Budget:** Law firms have larger software budgets
3. **Higher Expectations:** Attorneys expect premium tools
4. **Higher Liability:** More complex use cases require more support

### Market Comparison

**Depo-Pro:**
- Comparable to: Legal transcription services ($50-150/hour)
- Value: Time savings, accuracy, professional tools

**Attorney Review:**
- Comparable to: Trial prep software ($200-500/user/month)
- Value: Organization, efficiency, trial readiness

## 📊 PRICING STRATEGY

### Freemium? ❌ No

**Why:** Legal-tech requires trust and commitment. Free tiers attract wrong customers.

### Trial? ✅ Yes

**Recommendation:**
- **Depo-Pro:** 14-day free trial (no credit card)
- **Attorney Review:** 30-day free trial (credit card required)

### Annual Discount? ✅ Yes

**Recommendation:**
- 10% discount for annual prepayment
- 15% discount for 2-year commitment

### Volume Discounts? ✅ Yes

**Recommendation:**
- 5+ users: 10% discount
- 10+ users: 15% discount
- 25+ users: 20% discount

## 📝 IMPLEMENTATION NOTES

### Billing System
- Use Stripe for payment processing
- Separate products = separate subscriptions
- Can bundle later (same account, different line items)

### Feature Gating
- Use role-based access (already implemented)
- Can add usage limits if needed
- Enterprise = custom limits

### Upgrade Path
- Clear upgrade prompts
- Show value of next tier
- Make it easy to upgrade
