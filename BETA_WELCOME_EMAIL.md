### 📚 Getting Started:

**For Court Reporters:**
1. Visit the website and create your account
2. Verify your email (check spam folder if needed)
3. Create your first job with case details
4. Upload an audio file (MP3, WAV, or M4A)
5. Click "Start Transcription" and wait 3-5 minutes
6. Review the AI-generated transcript
7. Assign to a scopist (or review yourself)
8. Export to DOCX/PDF when complete

**For Scopists:**
1. Visit the website and create your account
2. You'll receive job assignments from reporters
3. Use the review interface to correct flagged words
4. Click timestamps to jump to audio
5. Use keyboard shortcuts for efficiency (↑↓ Space Enter R)
6. Mark words as reviewed when done
7. Complete review when finished
